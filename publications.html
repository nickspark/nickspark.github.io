Bridging Chemical Literature and Molecular Graphs: LLM-Augmented Enantioselectivity Prediction for Iridium-Catalyzed Asymmetric C–H Borylation

Zhe Wang, Baohua Zhang, Yi Isaac Yang, Yuan Yao, Yinwu Li

Advanced Science, 2026

Summary

A Massively Scalable Ligand-Protein Dissociation Dynamic Database Derived from Atomistic Molecular Modelling

Maodong Li, Dechin Chen, Zhijun Pan, Zhe Wang, Yi Isaac Yang

arXiv:2604.06761, 2026

Summary

DD-03B provides all-atom dissociation trajectories for 19,037 ligand–protein complexes, totaling about 0.3 billion simulation frames. The dataset supports research on drug–protein dissociation kinetics and generative models of unbinding.

Zhe Wang, Zijing Liu, Chencheng Xu, Yuan Yao

IEEE International Conference on Bioinformatics and Biomedicine (BIBM), 2025

Summary
